## Rationale

This framework is designed to maximize the diffusion of AI safety principles.
Commercial use is permitted to ensure the principles can be adopted by all AI developers, including commercial entities.
The ShareAlike clause ensures that any derivative work must retain the same open license, creating a viral propagation effect — the more TU-8 is used, the further its principles spread.

### Additional Clause: L0 Immutability

While this license permits derivatives, the L0 (Genetic Lock) layer of the TU-8 architecture is immutable by design.
Modifying L0 constitutes a fundamental violation of the framework's safety guarantees (see formal proof).
This is an architectural constraint, not a legal one — L0 is read-only at the implementation level.
